Choosing your template

Employees

Your card's look — its template, which the portal calls a "design" — can be changed from the portal, if your company allows it.

Where to change it

Sign in to the portal and open My cards. Find the row for the address you want to change, and press Change design. A gallery opens in place, filterable by Company, Seasonal, or Basic, each with a live preview of your own card in that design.

Open the employee portal →

Why you might not see a choice

Design choice is set per company. Your client admin controls whether employees can choose at all, how many designs are offered, and a cap on how many you can keep across all your addresses (picking the same design on two addresses only counts once toward that cap). If you don't see a choice, one of a few things may be true: your company has turned design changes off entirely, or your admin has assigned and locked a specific design for that address — in which case you'll see "Locked by your admin" instead of a picker. Either way, your card still has a design; your admin just picked it.

Aliases and shared mailboxes

An alias has its own row in My cards and its own design choice, including an option to just follow your main card's design instead of picking separately — see Aliases. A claimed shared mailbox also gets its own row — see Shared mailboxes.
