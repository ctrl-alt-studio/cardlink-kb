Managing templates

Client admins

As a client admin, you control which templates your company uses, and whether employees get any say.

How a card's template is decided

CardLink checks these in order and uses the first match:

  1. The template the person picked for themselves (for a shared mailbox, the one its current claimant picked, or one you've assigned to the mailbox)
  2. For shared mailboxes only: your domain's "Default for Shared Mailboxes"
  3. Your domain's default template
  4. The standard CardLink template

If a template gets deleted, anything using it just falls through to the next one in this list.

What you can set, and where

SettingWhere
Your domain's default template, and the default for shared mailboxesAdmin console, Domains tab
Whether employees may choose their own template, and how many they can keepAdmin console, Self-Service tab
Which templates are offered for employees to choose fromAdmin console, Self-Service tab
Which templates are offered for a specific shared mailboxAdmin console, Mailboxes tab
