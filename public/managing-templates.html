Managing templates

Client admins

As a client admin, you control which templates your company uses, and whether employees get any say.

How a card's template is decided

CardLink checks these in order and uses the first match:

  1. A template you've assigned to that person, alias, or mailbox (optionally locked — see Assignments below), or the one they picked for themselves if you haven't
  2. For shared mailboxes only: your domain's "Default for Shared Mailboxes"
  3. Your domain's default template
  4. The standard CardLink template

If a template gets deleted, anything using it just falls through to the next one in this list. An alias with no pick of its own, and no assignment, follows its person's current pick instead — not the domain default — and keeps following it as that changes.

Assigning and locking a design

On the Studio plan, the Assignments tab lets you give a specific person, alias, or shared mailbox a particular design directly, and optionally lock it. A lock on a person also covers their aliases; a lock on a shared mailbox covers every current claimant. A locked employee sees "Set by your admin" in their portal instead of a picker. Each change can be undone right after you make it. See Template availability & specials for what each plan includes.
