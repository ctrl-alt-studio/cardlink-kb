Managing your clients

MSP / reseller admins

For each client company assigned to you, you can do everything a client admin can do for their own company — no more, no less.

What you can change, per client

  • Company name and contact details, allowed domains and the primary domain, brand color, logo, website, and support phone
  • Whether shared mailboxes and aliases are included for that company
  • Self-service template settings: whether employees may choose their own template, the cap on how many they can keep, and which templates are offered
  • Per-domain settings: default template, default for shared mailboxes, stage, colors, and buttons
  • Activating and deactivating aliases
  • Marking mailboxes claimable, and each mailbox's template pool
  • Card overrides, template previews, and triggering a sync (Sync now)

See Managing templates and Why a card shows "unavailable" for the details — they apply the same way whether you're a client admin or an MSP admin working inside a client's company.

What's off-limits

Some things are restricted for every admin, client or MSP, and are CAD-staff only:

  • Seat limits, custom rates, and a client's package details
  • A company's status (active, suspended, archived)
  • Creating, editing, or deleting templates in the shared catalog — you can only choose among what's already there
  • Another MSP's companies, or anything about your own MSP's partner record

A plan change (moving a client between Starter/Pro/Studio) isn't on that list — you can start one on a client's behalf from their Plan change tab, same as CAD staff can. It's logged as a support action under your own login, with a reason. See Plans, roles & plan changes for how the change itself works.

New clients

Setting up a brand-new client company is handled by CAD staff, including assigning it to your MSP. Once a company exists and is assigned to you, you take it from there using everything above. There's no self-service way to add a new client yet.
