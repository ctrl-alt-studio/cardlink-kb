Get started: employees

Employees

Your company just turned on CardLink. Your digital business card already exists. Here's how to find it, check it, and start sharing it, in about five minutes.

Open the employee portal →

1. Sign in

Open the employee portal and choose to sign in with Microsoft. Use the same work account you use for email. There's no separate CardLink password.

2. Find your card in My cards

You land on My cards. Your own card is the row labeled Main. It's already live, so you don't need to create or publish anything. Each row shows a picture of the card and a short line saying why it looks the way it does.

If you also have aliases or a claimed shared mailbox, they show up as their own rows.

3. Check what's on it

Most of your card comes from your company's Microsoft 365 directory: your name, title, phone numbers, address and photo. If something is wrong there, ask your IT admin to fix it in Microsoft 365. Your card picks up the change at the next daily sync.

If the directory has nothing for your mobile, office location or job title, you can fill that gap yourself with Edit details. Your name can't be edited. That's on purpose, so nobody can pass themselves off as someone else.

More detail: What's on your card.

4. Share it

Press Share on your card's row to get its link and QR code. Anyone who opens the link sees your current card, and can save you straight to their phone's contacts.

If you have more than one address, the one marked Primary is the one you share by default. Use Make primary to change which one that is.

5. Pick a design (if your company allows it)

If you see a Change design button, you can choose your card's look from the designs your company offers. If you don't see one, your company or your plan doesn't include self-service designs, or your admin has set a design for you. You'll see "Locked by your admin" when that's the case. Either way, your card still has a design.

More detail: Choosing your template.

Optional extras

  • A shared mailbox. If your team uses an address like info@ or sales@ and your admin has made it available, you can claim it under Shared Mailboxes and get a card for it too. See Shared mailboxes.
  • A physical card or badge. If you were given an NFC card or badge, add it under Devices and point it at one of your addresses. If you lose it, switch it off there.
  • Motion. Some designs move. If yours does, an Animate my cards switch lets you turn the movement off on all your cards at once.
Your card updates itself. When your title, phone or photo changes in Microsoft 365, your card follows. You never need to reprint or resend anything.
