Signing in to the admin console

Client admins

The admin console uses your normal Microsoft work account, plus one extra step.

Open the admin console →

Who can get in

Only people your company has authorized as admins. Your company's contact person is the first admin by default; they can grant admin access to teammates from the portal's Admin section.

What signing in looks like

  1. Choose Sign in with Microsoft and sign in with your normal work account.
  2. Enter a six-digit code from an authenticator app. The first time, you'll scan a QR code to set this up; after that, you'll enter the current code from your app every time you sign in.

This second step happens on every sign-in, even if your company already requires Microsoft multi-factor authentication — it's a separate check specific to the admin console.

Signing out also signs you out of Microsoft in that browser.
