My cards, in detail

Each row shows a thumbnail of the design that address is currently using, and why: your own pick, matching your main card, your company's default, or set by your admin. Press Share, Make primary, or Edit details right from the row.

Press Change design to open a gallery in place, with Company, Seasonal, and Basic filters. A larger preview opens in a pop-up over the page rather than a separate page. On an alias, you can instead choose "Use my main card's design" — it then keeps following your main card's design as that changes, rather than locking in whatever it was at the time. A tick for "Also use this design on my other addresses" applies your pick everywhere at once, skipping any address your admin has locked or where that design isn't offered.

If your admin has assigned and locked a design for an address, you'll see "Locked by your admin" there instead of a picker. If your company has turned off design changes entirely, there's no button at all — a note says so.

A "Picks used" line reads something like "You have picked 2 of 3 designs allowed" — the cap counts different designs across all your addresses, so the same design on two addresses only counts once. If your company's plan is about to change, an affected row shows "Leaves <date>" ahead of time.

Motion

Some designs can move — an entrance animation, or an animated/video background (no designs do yet, so this won't change anything you see today, but the switch is live). An "Animate my cards" switch in My cards turns motion off for every address you have at once (an alias follows its main card; a claimed shared mailbox follows whoever's card was actually tapped). Your company can also turn motion off for everyone on a domain, and your own device's reduced-motion setting is respected too — any of these can turn motion off, never back on if a level above has already turned it off. A card with no motion in its design is unaffected either way. If a card's motion loops, a small button in its corner lets a visitor pause and restart it.

Signing out of the portal also signs you out of Microsoft in that browser.
