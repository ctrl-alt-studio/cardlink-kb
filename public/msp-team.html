Your team

MSP / reseller admins

The My Team page is where you manage who else at your MSP has admin access.

What you can do

  • Invite a new teammate (they'll get the same invitation link, password, and authenticator setup described in Signing in as an MSP admin)
  • Disable a teammate's access, or re-enable it

You only see your own MSP's team — not any other MSP's.
