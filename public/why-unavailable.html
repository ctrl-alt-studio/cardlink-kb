Why a card shows "unavailable"

Client admins

CardLink checks every card once a day (and whenever you press "Sync now"). If a card no longer qualifies, it's marked stale and shows as unavailable until the cause is fixed and it syncs again.

When this runs

Automatically once a day, early morning. You can also trigger it on demand from your company's overview page in the admin console with Sync now — useful right after you've fixed something and don't want to wait.

Every stale reason, plain language

What you'll seeWhat it meansHow to fix it
Not in the directoryThe account was deleted, or is no longer a memberRestore the account in Microsoft 365, or remove the card if it's gone for good
No email addressThe account has no email setAdd an email address in Microsoft 365
Account disabledThe account is disabled in Microsoft 365Re-enable it
Shared mailboxes turned offYour company has shared-mailbox cards disabledTurn them back on in the admin console
Domain not allowedThe account's email domain isn't in your Allowed Domains listAdd the domain in the admin console's settings
Alias not activeThe alias was deactivated, aliases are off, or its domain isn't allowedReactivate the alias, turn aliases on, or allow its domain
Not returned by the last syncCatch-all for anything else the directory didn't returnCheck the account in Microsoft 365; sync again

After you fix it

Press Sync now rather than waiting for the next automatic run — a stale flag clears the moment a sync finds the cause resolved. This is reversible both ways: a card can go stale and come back as many times as the underlying cause changes.

Employees and portal users can't fix a stale card themselves — this always needs a client admin to resolve the underlying Microsoft 365 or CardLink setting.

Removing a card for good

If a stale card should be removed permanently rather than fixed (someone has left the company, for example), your admin console has a Purge action for that.
