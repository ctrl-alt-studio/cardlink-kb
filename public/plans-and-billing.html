Plans, roles & plan changes

Client adminsMSP / reseller admins

Your company's plan decides which template-management controls you get. Two admin roles split who can do what.

On your company's page in the admin console, Assignments, Plan change, and Roles each have their own tab, grouped with the rest of your company's settings in the menu down the left.

Plans

Starter, Pro, and Studio decide which design-management controls your company gets — never what an individual card is allowed to show. See Template availability & specials for the full breakdown of what each plan includes.

Roles

Within your company, three roles split admin responsibilities:

  • Super admin — administers the company day to day, but cannot touch billing.
  • Billing admin — handles billing and plan changes, but has no other admin rights.
  • Admin — handles day-to-day design work.

One person can hold more than one role at once, and your company always keeps at least one Super admin, one Billing admin, and one Admin.

A Super admin adds or removes a company admin from the console's Roles tab (CardLink or your MSP/reseller can also do this on your behalf, as a logged support action). A new admin starts out as a plain Admin — an existing Billing admin has to separately hand over the Billing role. Removing someone ends any session they already have open right away.

Changing your plan

A Billing admin or Super admin starts a plan change from the admin console's Plan change tab (CardLink or your MSP/reseller can also do this on your behalf, as a logged support action).

  • Moving up a plan is immediate.
  • Moving down is never instant — you get a 4–7 day notice (or it takes effect at your next invoice date, whichever applies). In that window, you review which designs the new plan no longer allows and pick replacements. Your company's default design must be replaced; anything else is optional. Only the designs the new plan drops stop applying — nothing else changes, and nothing is deleted.
Notices about an upcoming plan change show up in the console and portal itself (the "Leaves <date>" labels you'll see on affected cards) — don't expect a separate email about it yet. Starting a plan change here updates which designs are available; it doesn't yet update what you're billed — that's still handled separately for now.
